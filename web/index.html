    How to use

    1. Open photos (or drop them on the page). Click one in the list to work on it.
    2. On Basic, pick the style (Photo or Comic), the material and the size. The material sets contrast, brightness, gamma, invert and mirror for you.
    3. Check the Engraving view (the real laser dots; zoom in) and Material look.
    4. Download PNG and load it into your laser software.

    In your laser software

    Engrave the PNG at the same resolution it was made at (254 DPI = 100 lines/cm = 0.1 mm line interval) and turn the software's own dithering off (use a plain black-and-white or threshold mode). The image is already dithered; resampling or re-dithering it makes blotchy, boxy patches.

    Remove background

    Uses an AI model that downloads once (176 MB) and then stays in your browser. Removal takes a few seconds per photo.

    Tips

    • Mouse wheel zooms, drag to pan. 1:1 shows one laser dot per screen pixel.
    • Not sure about contrast or gamma? Burn a small test on scrap first.
    • Presets and your own materials are saved in this browser only.

    Privacy

    Photos never leave your computer. The page and the AI model are downloaded; your images are processed locally.
