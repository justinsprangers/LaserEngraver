      New here? The tutorial points out every tool on the page and walks through the whole process, with a sample photo to practice on. In Design mode it tours Design mode.

      How to use

      1. Open photos (or drop them on the page). Click one in the list to work on it.
      2. On Basic, pick the style (Photo or Comic), the material and the size. The material sets contrast, brightness, gamma, invert and mirror for you.
      3. Check the Engraving view (the real laser dots; zoom in) and Material look.
      4. Download PNG and load it into your laser software.

      In your laser software

      Engrave the PNG at the same resolution it was made at (254 DPI = 100 lines/cm = 0.1 mm line interval) and turn the software's own dithering off (use a plain black-and-white or threshold mode). The image is already dithered; resampling or re-dithering it makes blotchy, boxy patches.

      Remove background

      Uses an AI model that downloads once (176 MB) and then stays in your browser. Removal takes a few seconds per photo.

      Fixing mistakes: with Remove background on, the Cut-out view shows removed areas tinted red. Pick a tool above the preview and paint on the mistake:

      • Smart keep (green, K): paint on part of the subject that was removed; it brings back the whole connected region. It only ever adds.
      • Smart remove (red, R): paint on background that was kept; it removes the whole connected region. It only ever takes away.
      • Brush keep / erase (blue / orange, B / E): change exactly the pixels you paint, for touch-ups after the smart tools.

      Fixes apply in order. Undo (Ctrl+Z) removes the last one. [ and ] change the brush size; right-drag or Space+drag pans while a tool is active. Fixes stay with the photo until you close it or reload the page.

      Design mode

      Switch Photos | Design (top left) to lay out photos, logos, vector files (SVG, DXF), text, arc text and shapes on one canvas, then Save engraving: the engraving PNG plus cut (red) and score (blue) files, or one all-in-one SVG / PDF.

      • Add (left) puts items on the canvas. Shapes: pick one, then drag on the canvas (Ctrl = square / circle, Shift = from the center, Esc cancels).
      • Drag to move (edges snap; Alt = no snapping), corner squares resize, the circle on top rotates (Shift = 15° steps). Ctrl+click picks the item underneath.
      • Layer tab: position, size, and Engrave / Score / Cut for the selected item; vector files get a job per color. Photo layers use the Basic / Photo / Comic tabs.
      • Design tab: canvas size, material, DPI, export options and a test copy.
      • Layout view is fast for arranging; Design view shows the real engraving.
      • Your design is kept in this browser. Save project writes a .lep file that also opens in the desktop app. PDF and Illustrator files need the desktop app.

      Keys

      • Both modes: wheel zooms, drag empty space (or right-drag / Space+drag) pans, F or 0 fits, double right-click fits, + / − zoom, C compare, Ctrl+S save (engraving in Photos, project in Design).
      • Photos: 1–5 views, K / R smart keep / remove, B / E brush keep / erase, P or Esc pan, [ ] brush size, Ctrl+Z undo a fix.
      • Design: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo, Ctrl+D duplicate, Delete, arrows nudge (Shift = bigger steps).

      Tips

      • 1:1 shows one laser dot per screen pixel.
      • The job time (bottom right) uses your engrave and cut speeds.
      • Not sure about contrast or gamma? Burn a small test on scrap first.
      • Presets and your own materials are saved in this browser only. The built-in materials can't be changed; use Save as… to make your own version.

      Privacy

      Photos never leave your computer. The page and the AI model are downloaded; your images are processed locally.
